PrivateAI MindToo

One private memory for your AI assistants.

You work with more than one AI assistant, but their conversations and memories are separate. Repeating the same context takes time. MindToo gives supported assistants a shared, editable memory on your Windows computer.

What it does

Handed over first

Before an assistant answers, MindToo gives it what it holds that bears on your message, from every assistant you use.

Honest about what it knows

When nothing it holds answers a question, it says so plainly.

Corrected everywhere

Tell any assistant it has something wrong, and the correction replaces it for all of them. The old version stays in the history.

Standing instructions

Pin what matters, and every assistant sees it at the start of every conversation.

Getting started

MindToo works with Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI, Cursor, Windsurf and the Claude app. Connect the assistants you use, restart them as the manual directs, then use Check to confirm messages arrive. Bring earlier conversations in from supported local histories and Claude or ChatGPT exports.

Download MindToo 1.15.0 for Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. The installer is signed by PrivateAI, Belfast Ltd. The help page explains how to check its published SHA-256.

Privacy

MindToo keeps your memory on your computer and searches it locally by default. It contacts Polar to activate and re-check your licence, and a wallet key’s provider when you press Test. If you configure an embeddings server, text is sent to that server. Connected assistants can receive the context you permit; their processing follows their own provider and settings. There is no MindToo account or MindToo cloud.

Controls

Only you delete

Assistants can remember, correct and archive. Deleting for good is yours alone.

Your controls

Pause it for every assistant, or lock it with a passcode. Give each assistant full, read-only or no access. Keep topics and conversations private from all of them.

Your keys

The API Wallet keeps your keys with Windows' own encryption. Assistants can see a key’s name, never its value.

Questions

Asked before.

Do I need to be technical?

No. Install it, open it, and connect the assistants it finds.

Can an assistant delete my memories?

No. An assistant can archive a memory, which hides it and keeps it recoverable. Only you can delete for good.

What if it gets something wrong?

Tell your assistant, and it corrects the memory for every assistant. The old version stays in the history.
